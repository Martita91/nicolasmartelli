About

Finance brain, builder hands.

I'm Nicolás, originally from Argentina and now based in Perth, Western Australia.

I come from finance and moved into applied AI by building. I don't stop at prototypes: I ship tools people use, from household budgeting with AI insights to voice-to-report apps for construction sites.

My focus is the gap between what AI models can do and what a business needs on a Monday morning: reliable data, clear numbers, privacy, and an interface people actually open.

On Instagram I share how to build with AI, step by step, for founders who don't code.
