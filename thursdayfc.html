Thursday F.C.: the app that runs my weekly football group

From "who's playing?" to balanced teams, results, stats and paid match entry, in one mobile web app the group opens every week.

By Nicolás Martelli · 4 min read

At a glance

What
A mobile web app for a weekly social football group in Perth
My role
Product and build
Stack
React, TanStack Start, Tailwind, Supabase (auth, Postgres, realtime), Stripe Checkout, Vercel
Status
Live at thursdayfc.au, with every match since the app started recorded in it

1. A group chat is a bad spreadsheet

Every week the same questions came up in the chat: who is in, are the teams fair, what was the score last time, and who still owes for the pitch. Thursday F.C. answers all of them in one place, on the phone, in a couple of taps.

2. What it does

  • Attendance: players tap PLAY to confirm, and the roster fills itself.
  • Balanced teams: one tap splits the confirmed players into two even teams (more on how below).
  • Results: each match keeps the score, the scorers, the man of the match and both line-ups.
  • Stats and rankings: points, goals, MVPs, win rate and caps, by month or year. Each player has a profile with the positions they play, their best teammates and their toughest opponents.
  • Payments: match entry is paid through Stripe Checkout inside the app, so nobody chases cash.

3. How the teams are balanced

Balancing by skill alone sounds fair, but it can put three keepers on one side. So the algorithm is position first: it generates many candidate splits, keeps the ones with the most even spread of goalkeepers, defenders, midfielders and forwards, and only then uses skill to break ties. Skill is each player's win rate, pulled towards 50% when they have played only a few games, so one lucky night does not make anyone a star.

Among the best splits it picks one at random. That is on purpose: re-shuffling gives a different line-up each time without breaking the balance.

4. How it is built

  • One shared club record: players, matches and settings live in a single row in Supabase. Changes save after a short pause and sync live to every open phone, with a guard so a phone does not overwrite itself with its own echo.
  • Managers by login: only signed-in managers can build the teams, checked against their login email, not against whichever player profile they pick.
  • Careful payments: test and live Stripe modes are chosen by the key itself, and a missing key stops the app instead of defaulting to live. The payment webhook is verified with a signature, and amounts and transaction details never leave the server.

5. See it

The home page, results and rankings are public. Confirming, building teams and paying need a player account.
