      How it works

      What the audit looks at

      What does it check?

      About 40 checks across four areas: SEO basics like titles, descriptions and canonical links; AI visibility; accessibility; and technical best practices.

      What is AI visibility?

      How easy it is for assistants like ChatGPT, Claude and Perplexity to read and quote your site: whether their crawlers are allowed, structured data, an llms.txt file and content that works without JavaScript.

      Which pages does it read?

      The home page, the pages it links to and the ones in your sitemap, up to 12 pages, plus robots.txt and llms.txt. It only reads public pages and stores nothing.

      Does it measure speed?

      No. It reads the HTML your server sends, so it cannot time how fast the page loads. The results link to PageSpeed Insights for that.
